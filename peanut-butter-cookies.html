Peanut Butter Cookies

Just three ingredients for chewy, nutty cookies.

0:00 / 0:00

Ingredients

Tap an ingredient to tick it off as you go.

  • Peanut butter – ½ cup
  • Sugar – ½ cup
  • Egg – 1 small

Method

  1. Mix all the ingredients together until creamy and smooth.
  2. Roll the dough into balls and bake in an air fryer at 175°C for 7–10 minutes.
  3. Take them out and let them cool.
  4. Serve and enjoy!
