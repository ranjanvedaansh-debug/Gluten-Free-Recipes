Bread Squares

Quick, cheesy air-fried bread bites.

0:00 / 0:00

Ingredients

Tap an ingredient to tick it off as you go.

  • Gluten-free bread – 3 slices
  • Cheese – 80 g
  • Onion – ¼
  • Tri-colour capsicum – ¼
  • Tomato – ¼
  • Olives – to taste
  • Pizza seasoning – 1 tsp
  • Oregano – 1 tsp
  • Chilli flakes – 1 tsp

Method

  1. Cut all the veggies.
  2. Divide the veggies between the 3 slices of bread.
  3. Sprinkle cheese on top.
  4. Air fry at 200°C for 8–10 minutes.
  5. Serve and enjoy!
