Quesadilla

A crispy, cheesy quesadilla stuffed with veggies and paneer.

0:00 / 0:00

Ingredients

Tap an ingredient to tick it off as you go.

  • Gluten-free flour – 50 g
  • Tri-colour capsicum – 50 g
  • Corn – 35–40 g
  • Carrot – 15 g
  • Onion – 35 g
  • Garlic – 1–2 cloves
  • Paneer – 60 g
  • Chilli flakes
  • Olive oil
  • Pizza seasoning
  • Cheese – 50 g
  • Sauce – 55–60 g

Method

  1. Gather all the ingredients.
  2. Dice the paneer.
  3. Chop all the capsicums.
  4. Cut the carrot.
  5. Chop the garlic.
  6. Dice the onion.
  7. Put all the flour into a bowl.
  8. Add a little hot water.
  9. Mix into a dough, adding more water if required.
  10. Knead the dough.
  11. Take half the dough and roll it into a flat bread/roti.
  12. Cook the roti on a griddle.
  13. Place a pan on the heat.
  14. Add oil.
  15. Add the garlic and toss it.
  16. Add the onions and fry for 2 minutes.
  17. Add the corn and carrot and fry for 2 minutes.
  18. Add the rest of the veggies and fry for a minute.
  19. Add the paneer and sauté it.
  20. Finally, add the sauce and seasonings.
  21. Take the roti and stuff the filling onto one half of it.
  22. Add cheese on top of the filling and spread it.
  23. Butter the griddle and roast until golden brown, flipping once.
  24. Serve and enjoy!
