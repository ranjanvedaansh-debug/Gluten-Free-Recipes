Ragi Cookies

Chocolatey cookies made with wholesome ragi flour.

0:00 / 0:00

Ingredients

Tap an ingredient to tick it off as you go.

  • Ghee – 2 tbsp
  • Baking soda – ½ tsp
  • Ragi flour – ½ cup
  • Almond flour – ¼ cup
  • Cocoa powder – 2 tbsp
  • Salt – a pinch
  • Milk powder – ¼ cup
  • Cornstarch – 1 tbsp
  • Powdered sugar – ¼ cup
  • Chocolate chips – 2 tbsp
  • Chia seeds – 1 tsp, soaked in warm water

Method

  1. Add the ghee and baking soda to a bowl.
  2. Whisk until creamy.
  3. Add the sugar and mix.
  4. Add all the remaining ingredients except the chocolate chips.
  5. Mix well.
  6. Add the chocolate chips and milk.
  7. Bring it together into a dough.
  8. Take a tablespoon of dough, roll it into a ball, flatten it and place it on a baking tray.
  9. Bake in a preheated oven at 160°C for 5–10 minutes.
  10. Serve and enjoy!
