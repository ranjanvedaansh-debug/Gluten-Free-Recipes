Pizza

A homemade gluten-free pizza loaded with toppings.

0:00 / 0:00

Ingredients

Tap an ingredient to tick it off as you go.

  • Gluten-free flour – 100 g
  • Yeast – 1 tsp
  • Salt – 1 tsp
  • Sugar – 1 tsp
  • Water
  • Olive oil – 1 tsp
  • Pizza sauce – 60 g
  • Cheese – 80–100 g
  • Toppings – tri-colour capsicum, pickled jalapeños, onions, olives
  • Pizza seasoning and chilli flakes

Method

  1. Mix together the flour, yeast, sugar, salt and olive oil.
  2. Knead into a dough using warm water.
  3. Let it rest for 1–2 hours.
  4. Flatten the dough into a circle.
  5. Bake it for 5–7 minutes, then take it out of the oven.
  6. Spread the pizza sauce over the base.
  7. Sprinkle over the cheese and then the toppings.
  8. Bake again at 210°C until the cheese is melted and browning.
  9. Serve and enjoy!
