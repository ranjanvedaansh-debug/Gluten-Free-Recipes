Orange Cake

A zesty orange cake with a sweet orange glaze.

0:00 / 0:00

Ingredients

Tap an ingredient to tick it off as you go.

  • Sugar – 200 g
  • Butter – 110 g
  • Orange rind – 8–10 g
  • Oil – 20 g
  • Orange juice – 20 g
  • Gluten-free flour – 185 g
  • Baking powder – 5 g
  • Salt – 3 g
  • Milk – 130 g
  • Eggs – 2
  • Orange juice – 20 g (for the icing)
  • Powdered sugar – 50 g (for the icing)

Method

  1. In a bowl, mix and rub together the sugar and orange rind.
  2. Add the softened butter and oil, and beat until creamy.
  3. Add the eggs one at a time, mixing after every addition.
  4. Add the orange juice and mix.
  5. Sift in the flour, baking powder and salt.
  6. Mix until there are no traces of flour.
  7. Add the milk and whisk just until smooth.
  8. Pour the batter into a greased and lined 8-inch pan.
  9. Bake in a preheated oven at 160°C for 35–40 minutes.
  10. To make the icing, whisk together the powdered sugar and orange juice.
  11. Pour the icing over the cooled cake.
  12. Serve and enjoy!
