Ragi Cupcakes

Rich chocolate cupcakes made with ragi flour.

0:00 / 0:00

Ingredients

Tap an ingredient to tick it off as you go.

  • Gluten-free flour – 55 g
  • Ragi flour – 55 g
  • Cocoa powder – 25 g
  • Sugar – 85 g
  • Butter – 110 g
  • Baking powder – 1 tsp
  • Eggs – 2
  • Vanilla extract – 1 tsp
  • Milk – ¼ cup

Method

  1. Put the butter and sugar together in a bowl.
  2. Beat the butter and sugar together.
  3. Add the eggs one at a time and mix.
  4. Add the vanilla extract.
  5. Sift together all the dry ingredients and add them to the mixture.
  6. Mix, then add the milk.
  7. Mix again.
  8. Line a cupcake tray with liners.
  9. Fill each liner ¾ full.
  10. Bake at 170°C for 10–12 minutes.
  11. Serve and enjoy!
