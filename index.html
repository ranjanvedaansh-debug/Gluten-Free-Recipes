What is gluten, and why does it matter?

Gluten is a protein found in wheat, barley and rye. It's what makes dough stretchy, so it's hidden in lots of everyday foods like roti, bread, pasta, biscuits and even some sauces.

Celiac disease is an autoimmune condition. When someone with celiac disease eats gluten, their immune system attacks the lining of their small intestine, which stops the body absorbing nutrients properly. It affects around 1 in 100 people, and many don't even know they have it.

Gluten intolerance (also called gluten sensitivity) causes symptoms like bloating, stomach pain, tiredness and headaches, but without the intestinal damage. For both, the only real treatment is avoiding gluten completely.

Why I made this website

Going gluten-free can feel really limiting. There still aren't many gluten-free options in shops and restaurants, and honestly, a lot of the ones that do exist just don't taste very good.

On top of that, not many people know what gluten is or why some of us can't eat it. That makes eating out, family dinners and even school lunches harder than they need to be.

So I created this website to share 12 gluten-free recipes I make at home, from cakes and cookies to rolls and pizza. Each one has a video, so you can cook along step by step. I hope it shows that gluten-free food can be easy, tasty and for everyone.
