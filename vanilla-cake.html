Vanilla Cake

A soft, fluffy gluten-free vanilla sponge.

0:00 / 0:00

Ingredients

Tap an ingredient to tick it off as you go.

  • Eggs – 3
  • Sugar – ⅔ cup
  • Oil – ½ cup
  • Gluten-free flour – 1 cup
  • Baking powder – 1 tsp
  • Baking soda – ½ tsp
  • Milk – ¼ cup
  • Vanilla essence – 1 tsp

Method

  1. Gather all the ingredients.
  2. Crack all the eggs into one bowl.
  3. Add the sugar.
  4. Beat with a hand mixer until creamy.
  5. Add the oil.
  6. Beat with a hand mixer until there is no trace of oil.
  7. Sift the flour, baking powder and baking soda into the batter.
  8. Mix well.
  9. Add the milk and vanilla essence.
  10. Mix well.
  11. Pour the batter into a greased and lined 6-inch baking pan.
  12. Bake in a preheated oven at 170°C for 25–30 minutes.
  13. Serve warm or cold.
